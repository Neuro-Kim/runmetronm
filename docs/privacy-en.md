# Pulse Run Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 12, 2026

This policy explains how Pulse Run and Pulse Run Remote (the "App") handle your information.

[한국어 버전](./privacy.html)

## 1. Information We Collect

The App **does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information.** It contains no advertising identifiers, no location access, no contacts access, no usage analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

The App does not declare the `INTERNET` permission and does not communicate with any external server.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is kept only in your device's local storage and is never transmitted externally:

- Metronome settings such as BPM, click volume, beats per bar, and tone profile

This data is removed when you uninstall the App.

## 3. Phone–Watch Communication

The App exchanges short control messages (BPM adjustment, play/stop, volume changes) with its Wear OS companion (Pulse Run Remote) through Google Play Services' Wearable API. This communication happens directly between your two paired devices and does not pass through any external server.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | Keep the metronome playing without interruption when the screen is off, via a foreground service |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Display the ongoing playback notification for that foreground service |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Prevent the device from sleeping while the metronome is playing |

## 5. Backups

On devices where Android's Auto Backup is enabled, the operating system may back up the App's settings to your Google account in encrypted form. Those backups are managed by Google; the App's developer cannot access them. You can disable Auto Backup in your device settings.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App uses only Google Play Services' Wearable API (for phone–watch communication). It does not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from that age group.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised along with the change.
